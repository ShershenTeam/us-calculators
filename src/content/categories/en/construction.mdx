Every tool in this category follows the same pattern: measure the area, pick a depth, choose the material, and read the quantity in the unit your supplier uses. Densities and bag yields come from manufacturer data and DOT specifications, and the reference examples are automated tests — see the [methodology](/methodology/).

Start with the material you are buying. If your project has several parts (a path plus a patio, a driveway with a turning circle), add each area in the calculator instead of estimating one big rectangle: the totals are summed for you.
